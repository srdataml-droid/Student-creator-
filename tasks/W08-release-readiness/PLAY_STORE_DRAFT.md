## Listing copy for the accepted product
- App name: Student Creators
- Short description: Discover original student videos, notes and campus creators.
- Full description:

Student Creators by Worthkind is a space for tertiary students to discover original explanations, connect with creators from their campuses and share their own knowledge.

Find student-created videos and notes, explore courses, follow creators and take part in campus conversations. Create original work and submit it for review before publication. Your work remains yours; access to a creator's work does not transfer its copyright.

Student membership is reviewed before protected features become available. Use examples to understand a topic, not to submit another person's work as your own.

The first release is invitation-only. Availability varies by institution.

Only retain sentences describing features actually accepted in the submitted build. No live classes, paid services, earnings, payout or payment claims until those flows and billing compliance are verified. No fake testimonials, student counts or ratings.
