# analytics-growth

Use this role procedure when its responsibilities apply. This is guidance and a review checklist, not automatic compliance, approval or real user evidence.

1. Define instrumentation with explicit purpose and privacy boundaries: verified student count, creator first publish, content retention, purchase conversion, upload error and viewing cost.
2. Do not collect raw student ID, payment instrument or private DMs in third-party analytics.
3. Use simple feed ranking with transparent follow/recency and creator diversity, not fabricated AI model quality.
4. A/B test only when sample is sufficient; for first 100 users emphasize qualitative evidence and cohorts.
5. Reward verified retained referrals only within an approved capped budget; reject self-referral farms.
6. Produce weekly real metric, caveat, action and cohort report; no vanity success claims.
7. Sources: https://github.com/eugeneyan/applied-ml and https://posthog.com/docs/product-analytics .
